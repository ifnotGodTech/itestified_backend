# Christian Testimony Platform Business Blueprint

Source of truth for the monetization/creator-economy vision behind Phases 21-27 in
`IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`. Provided by the admin 2026-08-16 (the original document referenced
as the source for Phases 21-27's proposal on 2026-08-04 — see that phase range's own
`Background:` notes). Saved here verbatim so it's an actual file in the repo rather than only a
remembered summary.

## Vision

Build a global platform where believers share written, audio and video testimonies that
strengthen faith, encourage people, and glorify God. The platform should become the world's
largest searchable archive of Christian testimonies.

## Core Product

- Anyone can create a free account.
- Free users can read written testimonies.
- Free users can watch/listen to public testimonies.
- Users can search, like, comment and share.
- Premium users unlock creator features and advanced experiences.

## Free Plan

- Read unlimited written testimonies.
- Watch public video testimonies.
- Listen to public audio testimonies.
- Search by topic, country, church, speaker and tags.
- Like, comment, bookmark and share.
- Follow creators and ministries.

## Premium Plan

- Upload unlimited video testimonies.
- Upload unlimited audio testimonies.
- Longer video duration and HD uploads.
- Offline download for personal listening.
- AI transcription to text.
- AI translation into multiple languages.
- Create playlists and testimony collections.
- Ad-free experience.
- Priority moderation and publishing.
- Live testimony broadcasts.
- Access to exclusive testimonies and interviews.

## Recurring Referral Model

- Subscribers receive a unique referral link.
- Referrers earn a recurring percentage while the referred subscriber remains active.
- Commissions stop immediately when a subscription lapses.
- Reward long-term customer retention instead of one-time signups.
- Example: ₦3,000 subscription with 15% recurring commission = ₦450/month per active referral.

## Creator Economy

- Verified creator profiles.
- Church and ministry pages.
- Followers and notifications.
- Supporter memberships (future).
- Creator analytics and engagement statistics.

## Prayer Follow-up

- Buttons: "Pray with me", "This encouraged me", "I'm believing God for the same breakthrough".
- Creators receive requests and can respond with encouragement and prayer.
- Creates community instead of passive viewing.

## Global Strategy

- Launch in English.
- Expand with AI translation.
- Support multiple currencies.
- Target Nigeria first, then Africa, UK, US and other regions.

## Guiding Principle

The subscription must be valuable because of the product itself. The referral program is a
growth engine — not the product. People should subscribe because the platform enriches their
spiritual lives, while referrals simply reward those who introduce genuine long-term users.
